Meet the act

The complete live act

Rosina & Co is a crowd-pleasing live act built around great songs, big energy and good vibes.

Available as a solo, duo, or trio, Robert Rosina leads on guitar and vocals with several full backing arrangements, with the option to add Danny Ross on vocals and guitar or bass, or Melissa Rosina on drums, or bring all three together for the complete Rosina & Co experience.

From familiar singalongs and shared choruses to driving rock, soul and dance-floor favourites, the set is shaped around the room and the people in it.

Whatever the lineup, Rosina & Co combines years of live musicianship with rich backing arrangements to deliver a full, energetic sound with warmth, character and soul.
